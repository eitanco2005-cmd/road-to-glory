Google search + free tools → self-serve PayPal checkout → automatic download.

   Hebrew and naturally lead to a paid product.
3. Rank the top 6 to build first, with reasoning.
4. SEO basics for ranking Hebrew pages on Google fast (RTL, lang="he", titles, schema).
Finish with `ops/reports/R-research.md` (top 6 + one-paragraph rationale). Commit and push
to branch `ws/research`. No pull request. Don't touch ops/STATUS.md.
