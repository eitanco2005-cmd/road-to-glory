# Report R — Product research (2026-11-17)

Full research: `research/products.md`.

## Top 6 to build first
1. **Wedding planner Excel/Sheets** (₪49) + free "כמה לשים בחתונה 2026" calculator
2. **Household budget Pro Excel/Sheets** (₪39–49) + free 50/30/20 calculator
3. **Osek patur 2026 tracker** with live ₪122,833 ceiling gauge (₪39) + free ceiling calculator
4. **Bar/bat mitzvah planner** (₪39, or ₪59 bundled with the wedding planner)
5. **Hebrew price-quote template pack** (₪29–39) + free 18% VAT calculator
6. **Renovation / home-build budget Excel** (₪39)

## Rationale
For every pick, the search found no ready-made Hebrew paid product, and each is a high-stakes moment for the buyer: a wedding, the family budget, the patur ceiling, an event, a quote to a client, a renovation. In each case a ₪29–49 file is trivial next to the money involved. Household budgets are the one category where an Israeli seller (Financa, ₪99–120) already proves people pay, so we can undercut. The wedding and osek patur picks pair with free calculators for queries that come back every year or every season. Those queries currently get stale articles rather than tools, and that is the cheapest way for a new site to earn Google traffic without any promotion. The planners share one spreadsheet engine, so #4 costs almost nothing after #1.

I dropped CV templates, psychometric/bagrut planners and a salary calculator. Free supply is saturated there (WPS, Microsoft, Campus IL, Bizportal and others), or the buyers are teens who can't easily pay via PayPal. I avoided tax-invoice templates on purpose: since June 2026, allocation numbers are required for tax invoices of ₪5,000 or more, so a template could mislead buyers.

## Caveats and choices I made
- No keyword-volume data (that needs an account). Demand is inferred from search-result saturation, paid competitors and media coverage. Re-rank after 2–3 weeks of Search Console impressions.
- PayPal Israel digital-goods micropayment rate (5.9% incl. VAT) needs an application. The owner should confirm the account's actual rate.
- The FAQ/HowTo rich-result deprecation dates come from secondary sources and should be verified on Google Search Central.
