## Decisions (made on my own)
- **"Free up to 80 guests" = 80 invitations (rows)**, not 80 people. One row is a family, couple or single person. Couples with 300 guests (~120–150 rows) hit the limit; small weddings stay free. Imported or added data over the limit is never deleted: the import stops at 80 and the unlock dialog opens.
- **What stays free in RSVP:** WhatsApp reminders, import, backup and the seating hand-off, because they create the habit and the traffic. Paid: no limit + Excel export.
- **No guest-facing RSVP form:** the site has no server, and keeping guests' phone numbers on the couple's device is a selling point. Guests answer in WhatsApp and the couple marks them. The FAQ says this honestly and compares it with paid RSVP call services.
- **Cost model shares the gift calculator's plate prices** (480/385, Thursday ×1.12, Friday ×0.82, luxury ×1.25, modest ×0.75), so what couples plan and what guests are told agree. Region: Jerusalem ×0.95 and north/south ×0.85 (Bizportal). Expected gift = 85% of a plate per adult guest, labelled as an estimate.
- **Mobile header:** with 4 nav links, "מדריכים" is hidden below 480px (still in the footer and the hub); otherwise the header scrolled sideways on 375px (caught by the seasonal suite).
- Product JSON gained an optional `"page"` (landing page when there is no `p/<slug>/`); `catalog.js` and `build_site.py` honour it. The RSVP app is its own landing page.
- Hanukkah home banner kept (it ends Dec 12, per status TODO).

## Bugs found and fixed along the way
- RSVP: editing "coming" and then re-rendering triggered a nested re-render during blur (DOM exception). The update is now deferred.
- RSVP + seating: saves are debounced, so leaving the page within ~150ms (as the hand-off does) could lose the last edit. Both apps now save immediately before the hand-off and on `pagehide`.

## Honesty / sources
- Fetching sources failed here (network policy blocks the Israeli news sites), so figures rest on cited search results: Attending 2026 price list (via Bizportal/Walla), Bizportal calculator (regional ratios), zap/N12 (67% to venue), Walla Mazal Tov / mit4mit vendor ranges. Vendor lines like suit, decor beyond what's included, rings, rabbinate costs, transport and the Friday factor are labelled "הערכה שלנו".
- Check-writing guide: facts cite Bank of Israel / bank terms via search. Bit/PayBox limits are **not** stated (unverifiable); the page tells readers to check in the app.
