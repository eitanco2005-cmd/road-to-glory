# Shop FAQ (Shop Manager → Settings → Info & Appearance → FAQ; add each question separately)

**Q: Is this a physical product?**
A: No. All items are digital downloads. Nothing is shipped. You download the files from Etsy right after payment.

**Q: Where do I find my files?**
A: On Etsy: You > Purchases and reviews > Download files. Etsy also emails you a download link. On the Etsy phone app, downloads can be limited, so open etsy.com in a browser if you don't see the button.

**Q: Do I need Microsoft Excel?**
A: No. Every spreadsheet also works in free Google Sheets: go to drive.google.com, click New > File upload, choose the .xlsx file, then open it with Google Sheets.

**Q: Will it work on Mac, iPad or phone?**
A: Yes: Excel for Mac, Google Sheets in the browser or app, and LibreOffice all work. On a phone it's easier to view than to type, so we recommend a computer for setup.

**Q: Which cells do I type in?**
A: Only the yellow cells. Grey cells are formulas that calculate by themselves. Start with the "Instructions" or "Start Here" sheet.

**Q: What paper size are the printables?**
A: Printable PDFs come in US Letter and A4. Print at 100% / "Actual size".

**Q: Can I edit the text?**
A: In the spreadsheets, yes: all names, categories and list items can be changed. The PDF printables are not editable.

**Q: Something doesn't work. What now?**
A: Send us a message. We'll fix it or send a new file. If we can't, you get a full refund (within 14 days of purchase).
