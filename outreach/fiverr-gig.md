## FAQ (short — Fiverr limits answer length)

**Do I need to pay for hosting?**
No. I host it free on Netlify or GitHub Pages. You only pay for a domain (~$10–15/year) if you want one.

**Can I edit it later?**
Yes. You get the code, and I'm happy to make small edits for a small fee.

**I don't have a logo or photos.**
That's fine. I'll use clean text styling and free, properly licensed stock photos.

**Is this WordPress or Wix?**
No. It's hand-built HTML/CSS: faster, more secure, and no monthly builder fees.

**Will it show up on Google?**
I set up the basics (titles, descriptions, structure). Ranking takes time; a Google Business Profile helps most for local search.

**What counts as a revision?**
Changes to text, colours, images or layout of the agreed design. A completely new design is a new order.
