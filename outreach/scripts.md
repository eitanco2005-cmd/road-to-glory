# Outreach scripts (the owner sends everything — personally, one at a time)

Ground rules
- One business, one personalised message. No bulk sending, no purchased lists, no
  scraping tools. Max ~15 new contacts per day so every message is real.
- Use only business contact details the business itself publishes (Google listing,
  their Facebook/Instagram page, a sign in their window). Never personal phone numbers
  or personal social accounts.
- Every email includes: who you are, a real reply address, your postal address (city
  + street or a P.O. box), and an opt-out line. Honour opt-outs immediately and keep a
  do-not-contact list. (CAN-SPAM applies to B2B email and requires an opt-out honoured
  within 10 business days plus a valid postal address — [FTC summary via Sendcheckit][cs1],
  [Salesforge][cs2].)
- If the owner is in the EU/UK: cold email to a *generic business address*
  (info@, hello@) under "legitimate interest" is generally accepted for B2B, but rules
  vary by country (e.g. Germany is strict) — prefer phone or walk-in there, and never
  email named individuals' personal addresses.
- Never claim past clients we don't have. Demo sites are called "examples I built".

## 1. Finding businesses with no / poor website (manual, Google Maps)

Time: ~45 min for 15 good prospects.

1. Open Google Maps. Search `{niche} near {neighbourhood}` — e.g. "plumber near Riverside",
   "barber shop {town}". Start with trades (plumber, electrician, handyman, cleaner,
   locksmith, landscaper), then salons/barbers.
2. Click each listing. Qualify it:
   - **No "Website" button** → Tier A prospect.
   - Website button goes to Facebook, Instagram, Linktree, or a "coming soon"/broken
     page → Tier A.
   - Website exists but on your phone: tiny text, no click-to-call, no hours/prices,
     copyright year ≥ 4 years old, not HTTPS → Tier B.
   - Good modern site → skip.
3. Prefer listings with **≥ 10 reviews and 4.0+ stars** — they're active and care about
   customers, so a site pays off for them.
4. Record in a simple sheet (keep it private, delete after 90 days if no deal):

| Business | Niche | Area | Tier | Problem seen | Public contact (phone / business email / IG) | Date contacted | Channel | Reply | Follow-up date | Opt-out? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

5. Write down **one specific detail** per business for personalisation: a review quote
   theme ("lots of reviews mention fast emergency call-outs"), their hours, a photo of
   their work.
6. Optional power move for Tier A: pick your 3 best prospects and ask the AI team to make
   a quick personalised mock-up from the matching template. Showing beats telling.

Channel choice: trades → phone (they answer between jobs, mornings ~8–9am or ~4–5pm).
Salons/cafés → walk in at a quiet time (mid-morning weekdays). Email when only an email
is published.

## 2. Personalised email

**Subject options** (pick one, keep it plain):
- `Quick idea for {Business} on Google`
- `{Business} — website question`
- `Saw your reviews, {first name if listed}`

```
Hi {name or "there"},

I came across {Business} on Google Maps while looking for {niche} in {area} — {specific detail, e.g. "38 reviews, and almost all of them mention how quickly you show up"}.

I noticed {specific problem: "your listing doesn't have a website, so people who find you on their phone only get a phone number" / "the website link goes to a Facebook page that hasn't been updated since 2023" / "your site is hard to read on a phone and has no call button"}.

I build simple one-page websites for local businesses: services, prices, hours, reviews and a big "Call now" button. They load fast on phones and there's no monthly hosting fee. Here's an example I built for a plumbing business: {PORTFOLIO_URL}/demos/plumber/

It's a fixed $149, ready in 48 hours, and you only pay once you're happy with the first version.

Would it be useful if I sent you a free mock-up of what {Business}'s page could look like? No obligation.

Thanks,
{Owner full name}
{Glory Sites} · {phone} · {PORTFOLIO_URL}
{Postal address}

If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.
```

Notes
- "Pay once you're happy with the first version" lowers risk for them. For a new seller
  that's worth it. Optional alternative: 50% deposit, 50% on launch.
- Keep it under ~150 words. No attachments on first contact (spam filters).

## 3. Phone script (trades)

```
Hi, is this {Business}? — Great. My name's {name}, I'm a local web designer — have you got 30 seconds, or are you on a job?

[If busy: "No problem — when's a better time to call?" Note it, hang up.]

I was looking at plumbers in {area} on Google and saw you've got great reviews — {detail} — but there's no website on your listing. A lot of people pick whoever has a page with prices and a call button.

I make simple one-page sites for local tradespeople. Fixed price, $149, done in two days, no monthly fees. Could I text or email you an example so you can look at it when you've got a minute?

[Yes → get their preferred number/email, send the demo link within 10 minutes, note follow-up in 3 days.]
[Not interested → "Totally understand, thanks for your time — I won't call again." Mark do-not-contact.]
[Already have someone / "Facebook is enough" → "Makes sense. If you ever want a page that shows up when people Google you, I'm local — have a good one." Mark do-not-contact.]
```

## 4. Walk-in script (salons, barbers, cafés)

Bring: phone with the matching demo open (salon/café), a simple card or flyer with
portfolio link + phone. Go at a quiet time; if busy, leave the card and go.

```
Hi! I'm {name}, I live nearby and build websites for local shops. Is the owner or manager around? — (If not: "Could I leave my card for them?")

I won't keep you. I noticed when I searched for {salon/café} in {area}, you've got {great reviews / a lovely Instagram}, but no website on your Google listing — people searching on their phones can't see your prices or book easily.

This is what I make — [show demo on phone, 10 seconds of scrolling]. Your prices, opening hours, booking button, photos. $149, ready in two days, no monthly fees.

If you send me your price list and a few photos, I can show you a free mock-up for {Business} this week. Interested?

[Yes → take their business email/IG handle, promise date, deliver early.]
[No → "No problem at all, thanks — have a good day." Don't return.]
```

## 5. Follow-up (one only for cold email; send 4–5 days after first message)

```
Subject: Re: {original subject}

Hi {name},

Just bumping this in case it got buried — happy to put together a free mock-up of a page for {Business} so you can see it before deciding anything.

If now's not a good time or it's not for you, no worries at all — reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up again.

{Owner name}
{Glory Sites} · {phone} · {PORTFOLIO_URL}
{Postal address}
```

Rule: maximum **one** follow-up for cold contacts. If they asked for something
(mock-up, quote), follow up up to twice more.

## 6. After a "yes" — mock-up delivery message

```
Hi {name}, as promised, here's a first look at a page for {Business}: {preview link}

Everything's editable — text, colours, photos. If you'd like to go ahead, it's $149 and I can have it live on your own domain within 48 hours. Payment by {PayPal/Stripe link / bank transfer} once you're happy.

What would you change?
```

## 7. Referral ask — friends & family

Send personally (WhatsApp/text/DM), one by one, to ~20–30 people. Not a group blast.

```
Hey {name}! Small favour — I've started building websites for local businesses (one-page sites, $149, ready in 2 days, no monthly fees). Here's my portfolio: {PORTFOLIO_URL}

Do you know anyone with a small business — a plumber, hairdresser, café, cleaner, personal trainer — who doesn't have a website or has a really old one? Even just their business name helps; I'll reach out myself and mention you, or not mention you if you'd prefer.

If they become a client, I'll send you a $25 thank-you (Amazon card or cash — your pick). Thanks so much!
```

Notes: if someone says the contact is a friend of theirs, ask the friend to make the
intro ("would you mind if I got in touch?") rather than cold-contacting. Pay referral
rewards only for genuine paying clients; the client doesn't pay more because of it.

## 8. Tracking targets (week 1–2)

| Activity | Per day | Week total |
|---|---|---|
| New prospects qualified | 15 | ~75 |
| Personal contacts (call / walk-in / email) | 10 | ~50 |
| Friends & family referral asks | 5 | ~25 |
| Expected positive replies (5–10%) | — | 3–5 |
| Expected clients | — | 1–2 |

If after ~60 contacts there are zero positive replies, switch niche or channel (per
STRATEGY kill rule, day 10).
