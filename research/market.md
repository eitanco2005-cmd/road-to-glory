third-party blogs or marketplace scrapes, not official data, so treat numbers as
ranges. Fee figures should be re-checked in each platform's help centre before
the owner sets prices.

## 3. Which niches convert

No source has real conversion data by niche; this is consensus opinion.

- **Home services (plumbers, electricians, roofers, HVAC, cleaners)** — most-cited
  best niche: high job values (one new customer pays for the site), often weak or
  no sites, buyers search "plumber near me" on phones. ([leadsbylocation][niches], [peerlist][niches2])
- **Salons / barbers / beauty** — one unverified table puts no-website rate at ~23%
  (vs plumbers ~19%, restaurants ~12%). Easy to reach (walk-in), visual, but smaller
  budgets; many rely on Instagram + a booking app. ([truehost][truehost])
- **Dentists / med spas / clinics** — reputation-sensitive, close faster, higher budgets,
  but more likely to already have a site. ([lindo.ai][lindo])
- **Restaurants / cafés** — split opinion: easy to approach, thin margins, often
  "fine with Instagram". ([leadsbylocation][niches])

Context: ~17% of US small businesses still have no website and ~28% of those have
no online presence at all ([Clutch 2025 via BusinessWire][clutch]). Many more have
a dated or broken site — the "poor website" pool is bigger than the "no website" one.

**Recommendation:** lead with **trades/home services** (plumber demo already exists in
brief A), second **salons/barbers** for walk-in outreach. Skip restaurants as primary.

## 4. What top gigs include

From sampled Fiverr landing-page gigs ([fvgig1], [fvgig2], [fvgig3]) and Fiverr's package
docs ([Fiverr help: packages][fvpkg]):

- 3 tiers (Basic / Standard / Premium) — Fiverr allows exactly up to 3.
- Delivery 1–3 days (Basic) up to 7–10 days (Premium); "extra-fast delivery" sold as an add-on.
- Revisions 1 / 2 / 3–5 by tier; explicit "a new design direction is not a revision".
- Responsive/mobile, contact form, basic on-page SEO, social links, speed optimisation.
- Section count as the scaling unit (e.g. up to 4 sections vs up to 6).
- Upsells: copywriting, logo, extra page, Google Business Profile setup, domain/hosting setup.

Our edge to put up front: **48-hour delivery, fixed price, free hosting (no monthly fee),
you own the code, real demo sites to look at.**

## 5. Realistic first month for a new seller

- No authoritative data. Forum and blog consensus: new Fiverr sellers rank low until
  they have reviews; many report **0 orders in the first 2+ weeks**
  ([Fiverr community][fvcomm], [fiverrlaunchpad][fvlaunch]). Common advice: narrow
  gig, competitive starter price, reply in < 1 hour, over-deliver on first orders.
- Upwork: proposals cost Connects; a new profile might need 20+ proposals per job won
  ([jobbers.io][jobbers]). Treat as a slow channel for month 1.
- Template products: early sales come from the seller's own promotion, not marketplace
  discovery. One self-reported case: a single HTML template made $647 / 89 sales in its
  first 30 days, driven by one tweet ([Medium case][tmpl1]); another seller on IH sold one
  $80 bundle and pivoted ([IndieHackers][tmpl2]). Expect $0–$300 from the pack in month 1
  unless the owner has an audience.

**Realistic 30-day planning numbers (my estimate):**

| Channel | Pessimistic | Expected | Optimistic |
|---------|-------------|----------|------------|
| Direct outreach (Google Maps + walk-in, ~60 contacts) | 1 client | 3 clients | 5 clients |
| Friends & family referrals | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| Fiverr | 0 | 1 | 3 |
| Upwork | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| Template pack ($24) | 2 sales | 8 sales | 25 sales |

Expected case ≈ 5 clients × ~$200 avg + 8 × $20 ≈ **$1,150 gross**. The $1,000 goal
depends mostly on **direct outreach**, not marketplaces.

## 6. Best channels for a seller with no reviews (ranked)

1. **Warm network** — friends/family who own or know a business. Highest trust, no fees.
2. **Direct local outreach** — Google Maps manual prospecting → personalised email,
   phone, or walk-in with a mock-up/demo on a phone. Generic "your site could be better"
   emails get deleted; specific ones work ([leadsbylocation: bad websites][badsites]).
   Fixed-fee packages close faster than hourly quotes ([lindo.ai][lindo]).
3. **Fiverr** — list on day 5 so reviews start accumulating; low cost to keep it live.
4. **Upwork** — Project Catalog listing (passive) rather than burning Connects on bids.
5. **Communities** for the template pack (see `marketing/launch-posts.md`).

## 7. Pricing recommendation

- **Direct clients: keep $149 Starter / $299 Standard.** It is well under typical
  freelance landing-page rates ($300–$1,500), so it's an easy yes for a small business,
  and a fixed price removes negotiation. Add a **$49 add-on** (Google Business Profile
  setup + domain connection) and an optional **$15/month care plan** (edits, uptime) —
  recurring revenue helps month 2.
- **Fiverr: launch lower, then raise.** With zero reviews, $149 Basic will be skipped.
  Use **$79 Basic / $149 Standard / $299 Premium**, raising Basic to $99 after 3 five-star
  reviews. Net after 20% fee: $63 / $119 / $239.
- **Upwork Project Catalog:** $149 / $299 / $449 (Upwork buyers expect higher prices).
- **Template pack:** $24 (direct Gumroad link); consider a launch price of $19 for the
  first 48h or first 20 buyers — a real deadline, honestly stated.
