# English (US) keyword map — Stream A, Jewish-life planners

Researched 2026-11-17 by SERP inspection (US web search). No paid volume tool is available, so volumes below are
**relative estimates** (H / M / L) judged from how many dedicated pages, calculators and Etsy/Gumroad listings compete
for the phrase and how often the phrase recurs in titles. Re-check with Google Search Console after 3–4 weeks.

## What the SERPs show
- **Bar/bat mitzvah planning**: dominated by vendor blogs (The Bash, Easy Event Planning, Pleasantdale, klezmer bands),
  GoCanvas forms and one ~$10 Gumroad Google Sheet. No strong *spreadsheet* product with budget + guests + gifts +
  thank-you tracking in one file. → our gap: a real Excel/Sheets planner with a dashboard.
- **"How much to give for a bar mitzvah"**: a large, spammy SERP (many near-duplicate AI pages), plus a few trusted
  sources (ReformJudaism.org "why multiples of 18", MyJewishLearning, JewishBoston, YourTeen). A clean calculator
  that outputs a chai multiple and explains its sources can stand out.
- **Bar mitzvah cost**: no authoritative national survey; pages quote $10k–$40k (planning sites), $50k+ NYC/LA,
  Philadelphia Inquirer 2025 ($10k–$50k "low-key"), kosher catering $60–$150/plate. → calculator must be
  assumption-driven, show ranges, and say figures are estimates.
- **Passover seder shopping**: Chabad.org gives concrete per-person quantities (3 matzot per plate + 2 per other
  participant per seder; ~6–8 hand shmurah matzot per lb; ~1 bottle wine per person per seder; ~2 oz maror per
  person per night; 1 egg per plate). Only one generic calculator ranks. → strong gap for a "seder shopping
  calculator" by number of guests.
- **Jewish wedding checklist**: Ritualwell, Smashing the Glass, The Knot, Chabad, YUTorah PDF honoree list. Gap: a
  spreadsheet that combines budget + guests + vendors with Jewish items (ketubah, chuppah, honorees/kibbudim, tisch,
  bedeken, yichud, sheva brachot).
- **Holiday hosting** (Rosh Hashanah dinner, break-fast, Hanukkah party, Purim seudah/mishloach manot, Shabbat
  dinner): Kveller ("Planning a Rosh Hashanah dinner in 14 steps"), Hey Alma, Unpacked. No planner spreadsheet.

## Seasonality (plan publishing ahead of it)
- Hanukkah 2026 starts the evening of **Dec 4, 2026** → Hanukkah guide is the most time-sensitive page now.
- First Passover seder 2027: evening of **Wed Apr 21, 2027** (second seder Apr 22; Chabad / calendar sources).
  Search interest typically climbs from late February.
- Bar/bat mitzvah and wedding phrases are year-round.
