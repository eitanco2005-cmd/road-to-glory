How the calculator works

  • Latkes: 4 per adult, 2 per kid; about 9 latkes per pound of potatoes.
  • Per pound of potatoes: ½ onion, 1 egg, 2 tbsp flour or matzo meal, ½ cup oil.
  • Doughnuts: 1.5 per adult, 1 per kid; 4 cups of flour ≈ 16 doughnuts.
  • “Light” appetite = 75% of the amounts, “Hungry” = 130%.

These are estimates for a typical home party, not rules. Round up for hungry teenagers and down if there’s a full dinner too.

FAQ

How many latkes per person?

Plan on about 4 medium latkes per adult and 2 per child when there are other dishes. If latkes are the main course, choose “Hungry” (about 30% more).

How many latkes from a pound of potatoes?

Roughly 8–10 medium latkes (a heaping tablespoon each). The calculator uses 9, with half an onion, one egg and 2 tablespoons of flour or matzo meal per pound.

How many sufganiyot (jelly doughnuts) per person?

About 1.5 per adult and 1 per child. A batch made with 4 cups of flour gives about 16 doughnuts.

How much oil do I need?

For shallow-frying latkes, about ½ cup per pound of potatoes. For deep-frying doughnuts, at least 4 cups in the pot; it’s reused for every batch.

No. Everything is calculated in your browser.
