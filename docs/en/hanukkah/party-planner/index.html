FAQ

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It’s a regular .xlsx with formulas only (no macros): upload it to Google Drive and choose Open with Google Sheets. It also works in Excel (desktop, web and mobile), Numbers and LibreOffice.

How does it know how many latkes I need?

It counts confirmed guests on the Guests sheet (optionally “maybe” too) and multiplies by editable per-person amounts: 4 latkes per adult and 2 per kid, about 9 latkes per pound of potatoes.

I make my own sufganiyot. Does it help?

Choose “Make” and it works out batches of dough (4 cups flour ≈ 16 doughnuts) and how much flour, yeast, sugar, eggs, milk, butter, jelly and oil to buy.

What’s the 8-night gift tracker?

One row per gift: night, who it’s for, the gift, budget, actual cost and status. You see totals per night and budget vs. spent on a chart.

Are the prices real?

No, they are example prices so the totals work from the start. Replace them with your local prices.
