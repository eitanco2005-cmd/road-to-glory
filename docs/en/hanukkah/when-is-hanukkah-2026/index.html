When Is Hanukkah 2026? Dates, Lighting Order and Blessings

Updated: November 17, 2026

In 2026 Hanukkah runs from sundown on Friday, December 4, to nightfall on Saturday, December 12. You light the first candle on Friday evening, December 4 (25 Kislev 5787), and the eighth candle on Friday, December 11.

For one menorah you need 44 candles in total: 36 candles plus a shamash each night — exactly one standard box.

Two Friday nights this year

Both the first and the eighth nights fall on Friday. On those evenings, light the Hanukkah candles before the Shabbat candles, and use candles big enough to burn until at least 30 minutes after nightfall (standard small Hanukkah candles often don’t last that long). On Saturday night, December 5, light after Shabbat ends; families differ on whether Havdalah or Hanukkah candles come first.

How to light the menorah

  1. Place the candles from right to left: night one has a single candle on the far right, and each night you add one to its left.
  2. Light the shamash (helper candle) and say the blessings.
  3. Use the shamash to light the candles from left to right — newest candle first.
  4. Put the shamash back in its place and sing Maoz Tzur.

The Hanukkah blessings

Blessing 1 (every night)

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה׳ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֹתָיו וְצִוָּנוּ לְהַדְלִיק נֵר חֲנֻכָּה.

Barukh atah Adonai, Eloheinu melekh ha’olam, asher kid’shanu b’mitzvotav v’tzivanu l’hadlik ner shel Hanukkah.

Blessed are You, Lord our God, Ruler of the universe, who made us holy with His commandments and commanded us to light the Hanukkah light.

Blessing 2 (every night)

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה׳ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁעָשָׂה נִסִּים לַאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בַּיָּמִים הָהֵם בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה.

Barukh atah Adonai, Eloheinu melekh ha’olam, she’asah nisim la’avoteinu bayamim haheim bazman hazeh.

Blessed are You, Lord our God, Ruler of the universe, who performed miracles for our ancestors in those days, at this season.

Blessing 3 (first night only)

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה׳ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁהֶחֱיָנוּ וְקִיְּמָנוּ וְהִגִּיעָנוּ לַזְּמַן הַזֶּה.

Barukh atah Adonai, Eloheinu melekh ha’olam, shehecheyanu v’kiy’manu v’higiyanu lazman hazeh.

Blessed are You, Lord our God, Ruler of the universe, who has kept us alive, sustained us and brought us to this season.

Some say “l’hadlik ner Hanukkah” in the first blessing. In the Hebrew above the Divine Name is abbreviated (ה׳); say it as usual when reciting.

FAQ

When does Hanukkah start in 2026?

At sundown on Friday, December 4, 2026. The eighth and last candle is lit on Friday, December 11, and Hanukkah ends at nightfall on Saturday, December 12.

Is Hanukkah on the same date every year?

It always starts on 25 Kislev in the Hebrew calendar, which moves against the regular calendar. In 2025 it started December 14; in 2026 it starts December 4.

How many candles do I need for Hanukkah?

44 per menorah: 1+2+…+8 = 36 candles, plus a shamash on each of the eight nights.
