Passover seder checklist (printable)

Updated 2026-11-18

Everything to tick off before the first seder: the countdown, the seder plate, the table and the shopping list. Passover 2027 begins at sundown on Wednesday, April 21, 2027.

This is the short, printable version. If it’s your first seder, read how to host a Passover seder first. Ritual items follow the lists published by Chabad.org; customs differ between families and communities, so adapt freely and ask your rabbi about anything halachic.

4–6 weeks before

  • Guest list for each seder (adults / kids), and who brings what.
  • Haggadot: one per guest, ideally the same edition.
  • Shmurah matzah ordered (it can sell out close to the holiday).
  • Menu planned, including any guests’ dietary needs.
  • Kosher-for-Passover wine and grape juice listed (see quantities below).
  • Plan for chametz: what to use up, store or give away.

1–2 weeks before

  • Non-perishable shopping done (matzah, wine, matzo meal, canned goods).
  • Kitchen cleaning plan and dates; kashering plan if you kasher.
  • Extra chairs, table and pillows (for leaning) borrowed.
  • Seder roles assigned: Four Questions, readings, afikoman hunt.
  • Afikoman prize bought.

2–3 days before

  • Fresh shopping: romaine, horseradish, parsley, apples, eggs, produce.
  • Make-ahead cooking: soup, brisket, charoset.
  • Table linens washed; dishes and glasses counted.

The night before

  • Search for chametz (bedikat chametz), if you observe it.
  • Eggs hard-boiled; horseradish grated (keep covered).
  • Shank bone or chicken neck roasted.
  • Salt water mixed (about 1–2 tablespoons of salt in 2 cups of water, per Chabad.org).

The seder plate

  • Zeroa: roasted shank bone or chicken neck.
  • Beitzah: hard-boiled (often roasted) egg.
  • Maror: bitter herbs, usually horseradish and/or romaine.
  • Charoset: apples, nuts and wine, or your family recipe.
  • Karpas: parsley, celery, onion or boiled potato, by custom.
  • Chazeret: second bitter herb (often romaine).

The table

  • Three whole matzot, covered, at the leader’s place (plus matzah for everyone).
  • Wine or grape juice for four cups each.
  • Elijah’s cup (and Miriam’s cup, if your family uses one).
  • Salt water bowls within reach of everyone.
  • A haggadah at every seat.
  • Pillows for leaning at the leader’s seat (or every seat).
  • Candles lit before the holiday begins.
  • Bowl, pitcher and towel for hand-washing.
  • Afikoman wrapped and ready to hide.

Put the soup on the table as soon as Shulchan Orech starts: after an hour of storytelling, hungry guests appreciate speed more than plating.

After the seder

  • Leftover matzah and wine stored for the second seder.
  • Note what ran out and what was left, for next year.
  • Haggadot and seder plate packed together.

Questions

What goes on a seder plate?

Zeroa (shank bone or chicken neck), beitzah (egg), maror (bitter herbs), charoset, karpas (a vegetable such as parsley or potato) and, in many families, chazeret (a second bitter herb). Customs vary.

When is Passover 2027?

Passover 2027 begins at sundown on Wednesday, April 21, 2027, so the first seder is that evening.

How much matzah do I need for a seder?

Chabad.org's guidance is three whole matzot per seder plate plus about two for each other participant, per seder. Buy extra machine matzah for the meal.

How many bottles of wine for a seder?

Plan roughly three-quarters of a bottle per adult for the four cups, per seder, plus grape juice for kids.
