Why a spreadsheet?

Apps come and go, and most event apps want a subscription. A spreadsheet is yours: it works offline, you can share it with your partner, parents or planner in Google Sheets, and it's still there for the next simcha. Our planners do the math (totals, balances, due dates, RSVP counts) so you only type what you know.
