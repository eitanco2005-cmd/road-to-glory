How to make a seating chart in 10 minutes

  1. One line per invitation. Paste from your RSVP spreadsheet: name, party size, side and group (family, college, work…). Groups become tables.
  2. Add tables. Round tables usually seat 8–10 (60") or 10–12 (72"); check with your venue. Add one spare table for late RSVPs.
  3. Auto-seat, then adjust. Auto-seat keeps each group together and respects "keep apart" requests. Drag to fine-tune; Undo goes back.
  4. Print the escort list. The A–Z list (name → table) is what the welcome table needs. Send the table map to your venue.
