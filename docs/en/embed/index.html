Add a free calculator to your website

Free calculators you can add to any website, blog or synagogue page with one copy-paste snippet. No sign-up, no ads, no cookies. They resize themselves and work well on phones.

How to embed

  1. Copy the snippet for the calculator you want (the “Copy code” button).
  2. Paste it into your site as an HTML block: WordPress “Custom HTML”, Squarespace “Code”, Wix “Embed code”, or straight into the page.
  3. Done. We keep the numbers up to date, so you never have to touch the code again.

Simple terms: embed it on any site, commercial ones included. Please keep the “Calculator: … by Tichnun Plus” credit line under it. Dark mode: add ?theme=dark to the URL. The calculator collects nothing; everything runs in the visitor’s browser.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No payment, no sign-up and no ads inside the calculator.

Will it slow my site down?

No. Each calculator is a small page (under 40KB, no fonts or libraries) that only loads when a visitor scrolls to it (loading=lazy).

Does it track my visitors?

No. No cookies and no tracking. All the math runs in the visitor’s browser.

Can I change how it looks?

Set the iframe’s width and starting height, and use ?theme=dark for dark mode or ?theme=auto to follow the visitor’s setting.
