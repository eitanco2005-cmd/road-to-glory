Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Seller: איתן כהן, trading as Tichnun Plus ("we", "us"), Israel. Contact: eitanco2005@gmail.com.

We collect as little as possible. No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, no mailing lists.

1. What we collect

  • Visit counts: the page path, the referring website (host name only), device type (phone/tablet/desktop), browser language and time. No name, email or personal identifier, and no cookies.
  • Purchases: the PayPal order number, product, amount and currency, to deliver the product, keep accounts and give support.
  • Email: if you write to us, your address and message, only to answer you.
  • Free calculators: what you type is calculated in your browser and never sent to us.
  • Local storage: your browser may keep your light/dark preference and a list of your orders so you can find your download page again. It stays on your device.

2. Payments through PayPal

You pay on PayPal's systems; card details never reach us. PayPal shares the transaction details and the contact details it gives sellers (such as the payer's name and email), as described in PayPal's privacy statement. We use them only for the order, support and accounting.

3. Service providers

  • PayPal: payments.
  • Netlify: website hosting; may log technical access data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts: the site font; Google receives your IP address when the font loads.
  • Google Drive / Sheets: if you upload a planner there, it's stored in your own Google account; we have no access.

We never sell or share personal information for marketing.

4. Retention and your rights

Purchase records are kept as long as accounting law requires (usually up to 7 years). You can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing us. If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you have the rights that law gives you; we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

5. Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top.
