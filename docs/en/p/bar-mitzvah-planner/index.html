Questions

Does it work for a bat mitzvah too?

Yes. Every sheet is written for both. Rename or delete anything that doesn't apply, for example the tallit line.

We're only doing a kiddush luncheon. Is it overkill?

Not at all. Leave the party categories empty and use the guest list, gifts and checklist. The totals simply ignore empty rows.

Does it tell me how much to spend?

No. The suggested % split is a common rule of thumb to start from; real prices depend on your area, guest count and vendors. Our free budget calculator helps you build a first estimate.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. The file uses only standard formulas (no macros, no add-ins), so it works in Google Sheets: upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. Formatting and charts can look slightly different. That way you can share it with your partner or family and edit together from any phone.

What versions of Excel does it need?

Excel 2016 or newer, Excel 365, Excel for Mac and Excel on phones and tablets. It also opens in LibreOffice and Apple Numbers (some charts may look different in Numbers).
