FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really live in 48 hours?

Yes — 48 hours from when we receive your completed brief and the first payment. Most previews arrive within 24 hours. If you need extra revision rounds, those add time only if you ask for them.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. You pay once. Hosting is free on platforms like GitHub Pages or Netlify. Your only ongoing cost is your domain name (around $10–15 per year), which you buy and own yourself.

I don't have a logo or professional photos. Is that okay?

Absolutely. We'll create a clean text logo and use tasteful illustrations or royalty-free photos. You can swap in your own photos any time.

What if I don't like the preview?

Tell us what to change — revisions are included (1 round on Starter, 2 on Standard). If we can't get it right after the first preview, you can walk away and only the deposit is kept.

Can I edit the site myself later?

Yes. You'll get a short guide showing how to update text like prices and hours. Or choose the $19/month edits add-on and just send us your changes.

Do you do online shops or big multi-page sites?

We focus on fast, focused sites for local businesses. If you need something bigger, ask — we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

How do I pay?

Secure online payment by card or PayPal. 50% to start, 50% after you approve the preview.
