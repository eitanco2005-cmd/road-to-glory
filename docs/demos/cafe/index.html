Our story

A corner café with a big kitchen heart

Ember & Oat started as a coffee cart outside the harbour market. Today it's a sunny corner spot with a wood-fired oven, a long communal table and the same promise: honest food, made from scratch, served by people who know your name.

We work with small local suppliers — our beans, milk, eggs and flour all come from within 30 miles.
